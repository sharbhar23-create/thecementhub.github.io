Our Story

Why We Are In
This Business

Aditya EnterprisesThe Cement Hub · Jaipur

We started Aditya Enterprises with a simple belief: that builders — whether raising a modest home or a major colony — deserve quality materials without being exploited, confused by opaque pricing, or kept waiting by unreliable suppliers.

What began as a small dealership grew into a full-service hub through word of mouth and the trust of our early clients. Today we stock 20+ cement brands and serve hundreds of contractors, builders and colonizers across Jaipur.

We are not just sellers. We are partners in construction. We understand the pressure of timelines, budget constraints and material shortages — because we have listened to our customers for years. Every policy we have built comes from that listening.

We grow only when our clients grow. That is the core of everything we do.

Founder’s Principle

Every bag we sell carries our name and reputation. We will never compromise on what we supply.

Community First

Supporting local builders, home owners and small contractors with the same attention we give large accounts.

Long-Term Vision

Building a regional procurement platform every builder in Rajasthan can trust as their first and final stop.

Our Commitments

Business Policies

Clear, customer-first policies ensuring every transaction is fair and transparent.

  • All quoted prices are final — no hidden costs at billing.
  • Quality certificates available for all cement brands on request.
  • Bulk buyers receive dedicated pricing tiers reviewed quarterly.
  • All deliveries come with a proper GST invoice and dispatch record.
  • Short supplies or discrepancies addressed within 24 hours.
  • Customer data and purchase history kept strictly confidential.
  • We do not deal in substandard or adulterated materials — ever.
  • Payment flexibility for established accounts and verified contractors.
Terms & Conditions

Clear Terms for
Smooth Business

📦 Orders & Delivery

  • Orders confirmed only upon written or digital confirmation
  • Delivery timelines communicated at time of booking
  • Pickup orders ready within 2–4 hours of confirmation
  • Delivery charges applicable beyond defined radius
  • Short delivery claims must be raised at time of receipt

💰 Payment Terms

  • Retail orders: full payment before dispatch
  • Wholesale accounts: credit terms on mutual agreement
  • Accepted: cash, bank transfer, UPI and cheque
  • GST invoices issued for all transactions
  • Overdue accounts subject to suspension of credit

🔄 Returns & Disputes

  • Damaged or incorrect goods reported within 48 hours
  • Returns accepted for unopened, undamaged bags only
  • Disputes resolved through mutual discussion first
  • No returns on custom or special orders
  • All decisions in disputes aim to be fair and amicable
